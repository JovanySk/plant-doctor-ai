# Sample image attribution

The sample photos in this folder come from Wikimedia Commons and are redistributed under their
original free licenses. They were resized to 1200 px wide by Wikimedia's thumbnail service; no
other changes were made. The `.json` files next to each image are the real, unedited responses
returned by `POST /api/diagnose` (model `gemini-3.5-flash`, thinking level `low`).
